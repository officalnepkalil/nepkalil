# NepKalil

NepKalil is a Nepal-focused platform in development, founded by **Pratik Acharya**, to help farmers connect with buyers and access useful services.

## Why NepKalil?

NepKalil began after Pratik saw farmers in Nepal struggle to find buyers and sometimes throw away their produce. The project aims to help farmers find market opportunities through accessible technology.

## Planned Features

- Agricultural marketplace connecting farmers and buyers
- Market prices and price trends
- Weather information and disaster alerts
- Farming reminders and activity records
- Customer support and inquiry history

Features are being developed and tested. Availability may change as the project progresses.

## Founder

## Future Plans

- **NepKalil Delivery** — delivery services, starting in selected areas
- **NepKalil Pay** — payment services, subject to the required approvals and partnerships

Both services are currently in the planning stage.
